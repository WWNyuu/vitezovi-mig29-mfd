MiG-29 WW MFD

A modern multifunction display for the MiG-29A in DCS: a datalink situation page, intercept steering, route navigation with time on target, fuel planning, stores, RWR and a full power-up sequence. All of it runs on the retrofit MFD of KRN MODS' modernized cockpit.

Add-on, not a replacement. Needs the DCS MiG-29A Fulcrum module and KRN MODS' "MiG-29A(M) Cockpit Modernization" V1.0.3, which you download from KRN on the DCS User Files. The physical MFD, buttons and UFCP are KRN's work; this add-on brings the pages and is shared with KRN's agreement.

The pages

SL. 2 – SL. 9

Situation awareness

A heading-up picture around your jet in the style of the F-15 SIT page. Friendlies, hostiles and your flight come over a simulated datalink built from what DCS exports, with sensor range, radar horizon and terrain taken into account.

  • Ground threats as rings, your route, markpoints and the cartridge's FLOT, borders and areas.
  • H1–H5 put the cursor on the nearest five hostiles; the datablock gives bearing, range, bullseye, altitude and type.
  • EXP zooms around a contact and follows it; the SYM rocker declutters in five steps.

Moving map

DCS's own aeronautical charts turn and slide under the symbols as you fly. Nothing is copied: the add-on reads the charts already in your DCS install, for Caucasus, Syria, Persian Gulf, Sinai, Iraq, Afghanistan, Nevada, the Marianas, South Atlantic, Germany Cold War and Kola.

One option in DCS's Special settings turns it on; the MAP button hides it in flight.

Picture and intercept

PICTURE groups the contacts into formations, nearest first, and writes the call out for you: BULLS 066/93, 7000, FLANK W, HOSTILE, 2 SHIP SU-27. CALL switches between bullseye and BRAA.

INTC solves the collision course to a target and puts it on a heading tape: steer the diamond to the centre and you meet head-on. The readout gives time to intercept, closure and the fuel you will have when you get there.

Advisory only: the autopilot, HUD, radar and weapons stay stock.

Navigation and time on target

The NAV page flies the cartridge's whole route, not just the jet's three waypoints. A steering bar gives the command heading back onto the planned leg, and every bearing on the SA page, HSI and UFCP is the same true value.

  • Times on target on any number of points, with the speed to fly on each leg and a speed cue.
  • One cartridge for the whole flight: tag points by member number and each pilot picks his route.
  • Typed-in waypoints in lat/long or MGRS, an EDIT page to move, re-position, add or delete any route point in flight, direct-to, auto sequencing and RSBN stations in range.

Power-up

SL. 10 – SL. 12

A display that starts like a real one

The MFD runs on the jet's DC power. On a cold start it stays dark until the battery is on and you press PWR. Then it boots: screen test, boot ROM, software log, the squadron badge, a built-in test checklist with your aligned position, and the cartridge choice.

  • Hold PWR for 2 seconds, then let go to switch off; it cannot be knocked off by accident.
  • A short power blink keeps your page; a real power loss, like a rearm, means a full restart.
  • Hot and air starts come up already running.

Cartridges and the DTC Planner

Pick the mission's data cartridge right after start-up: route preview, flight members, radio presets and drawings at a glance. Cartridges are DCS's own MiG-29 .dtc files, so the jet's navigation gets its waypoints, airfields and RSBN at the same time.

The Vitezovi DTC Planner comes with it: one offline HTML file with every DCS map built in. Plan routes, threat rings, FLOT and CAP areas, and save the cartridge with its drawings.

Install

The add-on contains none of KRN's files. Its installer checks your copy of KRN's mod, keeps the originals and adds what the pages need. Uninstalling puts KRN's mod back exactly as it was.

  1. Install KRN's modMiG-29A(M) Cockpit Modernization V1.0.3 from KRN MODS (DCS User Files), into Saved Games\DCS\Mods\tech.
  2. Run INSTALL.batDownload the add-on, unzip it anywhere and double-click INSTALL.bat. Close DCS first.
  3. FlyBattery on, press PWR on the MFD's top-left rocker, choose a cartridge. The pilot manual explains every page.
